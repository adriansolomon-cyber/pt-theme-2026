Questions

Answered before you ask.

Can I choose where my windows and doors go?+
Yes — all Grandmaster panels are modular. Windows and doors can be positioned anywhere during installation. No fixed layouts, no compromise.
Is the whole building pressure treated?+
Yes — every component. Cladding, framing, floor, and roof boards all go through a vacuum pressure treatment process that drives preservative deep into the timber.
What base do I need?+
A flat, solid surface — concrete slab, pad, or treated timber frame. We have a full base guide and our team can advise on your specific garden and size.
Can I add insulation?+
Yes — insulation kits are available at checkout. The 54mm studwork accepts standard UK insulation batts. Our Garden Room Ready and Retreat bundles include the full kit.
How long does it take to build?+
Most Grandmaster buildings go up in 4–8 hours with two people. The pre-built modular panels make it fast — no stacking, no mallet. Professional assembly available on all models.
Do I need planning permission?+
Most Grandmaster buildings fall within Permitted Development rights — single-storey, under 2.5m eaves, under 50% garden coverage. Our team can advise on typical scenarios.
